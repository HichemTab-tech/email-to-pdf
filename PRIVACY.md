# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

EML to PDF is a browser extension built by Hichem. It converts email files into printable documents entirely within your browser.

## Data we handle

When you choose an EML file, the extension reads it locally to display its headers, message body, and attachment names. The file and its contents are not uploaded or transmitted by the extension.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect analytics or telemetry.
- We do not operate a server that receives email data.
- We do not store selected emails after the extension page is closed or refreshed.
- We do not sell, rent, or share information.
- We do not request access to Gmail, browsing history, or websites.

Remote images in email HTML are blocked during preview to prevent tracking requests. Links remain user-initiated and open separately.

## Permissions

The extension does not request optional permissions, host permissions, Gmail access, or a Google account connection.

## PDF creation

Selecting “Print / Save PDF” opens the browser's print dialog. PDF output is handled by the browser and operating system according to their settings.

## Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the [project's GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HichemTab-tech/email-to-pdf/issues).
